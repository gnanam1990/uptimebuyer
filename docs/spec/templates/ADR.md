# Architecture Decision Record

## Record metadata

- Decision ID:
- Title:
- Status: proposed
- Date:
- Owners:
- Related requirement, invariant, module, and test IDs:

## Context

Describe the observed problem, constraints, current repository evidence, and the decision boundary. Separate verified facts from assumptions.

## Options considered

Document each credible option, including operational burden, security, failure behavior, reversibility, and impact on the core invariants.

## Decision

State the selected option and why it is the smallest complete choice that preserves the contract.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, newly introduced risks, and constraints imposed on future modules.

## Verification

List the tests, probes, and real-world evidence that can falsify the decision. Unexecuted checks remain `NOT_RUN`.

## Rollback or replacement

Explain how to reverse or supersede the decision without losing data, evidence, or auditability.
